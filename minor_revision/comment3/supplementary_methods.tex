\subsection{Information retention and alternative subgraph selectors}

We assess evidence compression at three separate levels: retained graph
structure, the representations supplied by the frozen graph encoder, and
generated answers. All comparisons fix the candidate identities and their
ordering to the original fuzzy input. For repoDB this is the original
raw/display-control ordering. Candidate ranking metrics are therefore invariant
by construction and are not used as evidence of information retention.

\paragraph{Source graphs and matched budgets.}
We distinguish the multiset of retrieved edge occurrences from the set of unique
directed, relation-labeled triples. Two uncompressed references retain either
the full multiset or only unique triples. The original fuzzy graph is read
directly from its archived artifact. For each query, all compressed alternatives
select exactly the number of unique triples in that artifact from the same
unique source-edge set. The empty-graph diagnostic removes local message
passing while retaining frozen global entity embeddings. These uncompressed
and empty references are not treated as matched-budget competitors.

\paragraph{Alternative selection strategies.}
The alternatives include uniform random sampling without replacement;
random ordering followed by candidate/query coverage; top-$B$ native-score
selection without coverage; native-score selection with a strict coverage-then-fill
pass; endpoint-degree top-$B$; query-centered breadth-first ordering;
query-personalized PageRank; terminal-personalized PageRank; shortest-path
preservation; and relation-stratified selection. Native scores are recovered
from archived features/scores, with exact triple and candidate alignment checks.
The strict native-score coverage control is not claimed to reproduce every
dataset-specific reservation pass of the original implementation.

PageRank uses an unweighted undirected source graph, damping 0.85, and either
the query alone or the query and present candidates as uniformly weighted
teleportation nodes. Dangling mass returns to the teleportation distribution.
Iteration stops at an L1 change below $10^{-10}$ or after 200 iterations.
Edges are ranked by the sum of endpoint scores. The shortest-path selector
considers candidates in their fixed order, adds one shortest path of length at
most three if the complete path fits the remaining budget, and fills remaining
slots by native score. Relation-stratified selection cycles over relation IDs,
ranking edges within each stratum by native score. Stable source-index
tie-breaking is used unless the explicitly seeded random ordering applies.
No selector accesses the held-out gold label.

Five random seeds are evaluated at the native budget. Additional strict-budget
curves use 20\%, 35\%, 55\%, 75\%, and 90\% of unique source edges, with three
seeds for random selection. The main CPU study contains 62 variants per
dataset/split across all five datasets and both validation and test. A separate
60-row native-implementation audit calls the original selector functions,
reproduces the archived edge sets at their native budgets, and varies only the
requested budget. It preserves and reports any budget excess caused by the
original reservation passes rather than silently imposing a new policy.

\paragraph{Structural retention definitions.}
Candidate coverage measures incidence to at least one retained edge and is
reported both absolutely and relative to source-covered candidates, including
top-, middle-, and tail-rank bands. Query coverage, unique-node retention,
relation-type retention, and direct query--candidate exposure are reported
separately. For $k\in\{2,3\}$, let $C_{\leq k}(G_q)$ be candidates reachable from
the query within $k$ edges in the source graph and let $P_k(G_q)$ be its
edge-labeled simple path witnesses of exactly $k$ edges ending at a candidate.
We compute
\[
 R_{\mathrm{reach},k}(q)=
 \frac{|C_{\leq k}(S_q)|}{|C_{\leq k}(G_q)|},\qquad
 R_{\mathrm{path},k}(q)=
 \frac{|\{p\in P_k(G_q):E(p)\subseteq E(S_q)\}|}{|P_k(G_q)|}.
\]
The walks are traversable in either direction; exact edge identities, relation
labels, and traversal signs are retained. Consequently these quantities measure
structural connectivity, not necessarily directed biochemical pathways. Typed
pattern retention measures whether each source relation/sign sequence of
length two or three still has at least one witness. A separate audit measures
retention of unique incoming edges within two predecessor steps of the query
and candidates, matching the two message-passing layers of the actual directed
encoder more closely.

Relation-frequency and node-incidence mass distributions are compared using
base-2 Jensen--Shannon divergence. Rare-relation edge recall uses relations with
source frequency at or below the per-query median. Explicit Gene annotations
from DRKG/repoDB namespaces and unambiguous Hetionet metadata support an
additional gene-intermediate path diagnostic; types are not inferred from
gene-like names in PrimeKG or PharmKG. Gold-incident and gold-reachability
statistics are computed only after selection and only when the gold is in the
fixed candidate set and the relevant source denominator is nonzero.

Any retention with a zero source denominator is undefined, omitted from the
mean, and accompanied by its eligible sample count. The reported means are
averages of per-query-row ratios, not ratios of pooled path counts. Quantiles,
complete-loss fractions, source-query-presence strata, and gold-coverage strata
supplement the aggregate results. Twenty illustrative cases are selected
post hoc by fixed low/median/high structural-retention strata; they are not chosen
for favorable generated answers or asserted biological mechanisms.

\paragraph{Frozen-model evaluation and numerical audit.}
The GPU study evaluates all 22 native-budget variants and six additional
strict-budget variants (native-score coverage, terminal PageRank, and random
seed zero at 35\% and 75\%) on the complete test split. The same frozen
checkpoint, prompt, candidate order, and deterministic decoding settings apply
to every selector within each dataset. We measure cosine similarity and
relative L2 change of query/candidate adapter outputs against both source
references. Generated-answer exact accuracy, invalid-output rate, top-candidate
copying, normalized output agreement, and the explicitly standardized
promote-to-front adjusted MRR are reported separately.

A numerical audit found nine nonfinite FP16 encoder outputs for PrimeKG source
graphs. FP32 encoder outputs were finite in all 4,200 source/fuzzy test-graph
cases audited, but some projected values exceeded the FP16 exponent range.
The final protocol therefore loads graph-encoder checkpoint weights in FP32
and LLM weights in BF16, checks finiteness before and after projection casting,
and performs neither clipping nor sample exclusion. Earlier FP16 prediction
caches are not reused. This precision change is applied uniformly across all
methods and is disclosed when comparing to historical results.

The existing encoder falls back to global/global embeddings for graphs with at
most ten edge occurrences; we explicitly report this condition at small
budgets. An encoder benchmark uses up to 32 distinct query groups, three
repetitions, warm-up calls, and CUDA synchronization. It includes graph
construction, encoding, projection, and output transfer, but excludes LLM
generation and must not be interpreted as end-to-end latency.
CPU selection-call timings start after source-context construction and score
loading; shared adjacency/path enumeration is excluded and ordering caches can
be reused. These descriptive timings are not a total-runtime comparison of
selection algorithms.

\paragraph{Uncertainty and scope.}
Paired query-cluster bootstrap intervals use 2,000 resamples so that rows sharing
the same query remain together. Random-selector means additionally receive
joint query-cluster/seed bootstrap intervals; seed standard deviations are
reported separately. These are exploratory intervals without multiplicity
adjustment. All methods, ratios, and seeds are declared before inference; no
test-winning configuration is promoted to a new default. Retention is measured
relative to retrieved source subgraphs and does not establish preservation of
all KG facts, expert-validated mechanisms, or clinical utility.
